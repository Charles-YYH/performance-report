Methodology and sources

How I measured it

IBKR balances, trades, and returns

The opening and closing values came from IBKR’s October 6 close and the November 19 account snapshot used for the original review. The daily NAV and return series imply a €3,500 contribution on October 30. I used position sizes, historical prices and available executions to estimate the holding-level contributions.

Currency translation

I converted the opening balance, the contribution and the closing balance at their respective ECB reference rates. The latest reference rate available for this review was November 18. The EUR/CNY line measures the change in RMB value of the opening IBKR capital and the contribution. The other IBKR lines express the estimated EUR investment results in yuan at the ending rate.

Outside investment estimates and scope

I estimated the two outside holdings using QQQ and Xetra-Gold price changes through November 18, converted to CNY. The estimates assume both positions were held throughout the period with no cash flows. Actual fund returns may differ due to tracking, hedging, fees, or trade dates. The report covers investments only. Bank balances and other household assets are outside its scope.

Benchmark and portfolio return calculations

I linked the estimated combined portfolio returns before and after the October 30 contribution, treating the contribution as start-of-day capital. The IBKR comparison converts its reported EUR time-weighted return into CNY using the same endpoint exchange rates. Index returns use the NDX and SPX closing levels from IBKR market data, converted from USD to CNY using ECB cross-rates.
