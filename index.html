Methodology and sources

How I measured it

IBKR balances, trades, and returns

The IBKR valuations came from the October 6 close and the November 23 valuation in IBKR’s performance series. The daily NAV and return series imply a €3,500 contribution on October 30. I used position sizes, historical prices and available executions to estimate the holding-level contributions.

Currency translation

I used ECB reference rates for the opening balance and contribution. For the November 23 snapshot and the daily change, I used the latest available reference rate from November 20. The EUR/CNY line measures the change in RMB value of the opening IBKR capital and the contribution. The other IBKR lines express the estimated EUR investment results in yuan at the ending rate.

Outside investment estimates and scope

I rolled forward the previously supplied outside holding values from the November 18 proxy marks using QQQ’s November 20 close and the November 23 Xetra-Gold mark, converted to CNY. The estimates assume both positions were held throughout the period with no cash flows. Actual fund returns may differ due to tracking, hedging, fees, or trade dates. The report covers investments only. Bank balances and other household assets are outside its scope.

Benchmark and portfolio return calculations

I linked the estimated combined portfolio returns before and after the October 30 contribution, treating the contribution as start-of-day capital. The IBKR comparison converts its reported EUR time-weighted return into CNY using the same endpoint exchange rates. Index returns use the NDX and SPX closing levels from IBKR market data, converted from USD to CNY using ECB cross-rates.
